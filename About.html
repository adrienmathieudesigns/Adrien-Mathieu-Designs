ABOUT ADRIEN MATHIEU

Originally from Portland, Oregon, Adrien Mathieu is a Lighting Designer and Artist living and working in Chicago, Illinois. Currently, Adrien is a senior at The Theatre School of DePaul University, working towards a BFA in Lighting Design.

"In my mind, meaningful art does three things: It makes us reconsider the world around us, it captivates us visually, and finally it is fun and fulfilling to create and experience."
- Adrien Mathieu
