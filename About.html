Originally born in Portland, Oregon, Adrien Mathieu is a Lighting Designer and Artist living and working in Chicago, Illinois. Currently, Adrien is a senior at The Theatre School of DePaul University, working towards a BFA in Lighting Design. Adrien is a hard working, quick thinker, who is always looking to learn somthing new.
